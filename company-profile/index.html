VoltaMission company profile

VoltaMission qualifies battery cell (18650, 4680 cylindrical, and pouch) and custom BMS suppliers for US and Canadian manufacturers. The company is based in Toronto, Ontario, Canada and works across North America and Asia.

What VoltaMission does

VoltaMission provides independent technical sourcing and supplier qualification for battery cells and custom battery management systems. It helps clients find and evaluate cylindrical (18650, 4680) and pouch cell suppliers, custom BMS vendors, non-catalog and small/R&D-volume cell and BMS builds, and global battery suppliers that are difficult to source through distributors or marketplaces.

Core services

  • Technical vendor qualification for battery cells and BMS based on cycle life, energy density, thermal-runaway margin, protection-circuit accuracy, cell-balancing performance, manufacturing capability, samples, legitimacy, quality risk, and origin-compliance concerns.
  • Hard-to-find battery cell and custom BMS component sourcing for hardware startups, EV builders, research groups, and manufacturers.
  • Cross-border sourcing support, including technically qualified cell and BMS supplier shortlists, overseas-side QC support, and bilingual English/Mandarin negotiation.

Who hires VoltaMission

VoltaMission serves US and Canadian manufacturers, hardware startups, EV builders, research groups, and procurement teams that need technical confidence in a battery cell or BMS supplier before committing — especially teams without an in-house battery engineer.

What VoltaMission is not

VoltaMission is not a battery manufacturer, reseller, distributor, marketplace, or broker. It holds no cell or BMS inventory and takes no margin on what it qualifies. Clients pay for engineering judgment, technical evaluation, sourcing strategy, and de-risked supplier introductions.
