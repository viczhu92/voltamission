VoltaMission company profile

VoltaMission qualifies global hardware suppliers for US and Canadian manufacturers. The company is based in Toronto, Ontario, Canada and works across North America and Asia.

What VoltaMission does

VoltaMission provides engineering-led technical sourcing and supplier qualification for hardware teams. It helps clients find and evaluate specialized components, non-catalog parts, small-volume and R&D-volume components, manufacturing equipment, and global suppliers that are difficult to source through distributors or marketplaces.

Core services

  • Technical vendor qualification based on specifications, manufacturing capability, samples, legitimacy, quality risk, and origin-compliance concerns.
  • Hard-to-find specialized component sourcing for hardware startups, research groups, and manufacturers.
  • Cross-border sourcing support, including technically qualified supplier shortlists, overseas-side QC support, and bilingual English/Mandarin negotiation.

Who hires VoltaMission

VoltaMission serves US and Canadian manufacturers, hardware startups, research groups, and procurement teams that need technical confidence before engaging a global supplier.

What VoltaMission is not

VoltaMission is not a reseller, distributor, marketplace, or catalog broker. Clients pay for engineering judgment, technical evaluation, sourcing strategy, and de-risked supplier introductions.
