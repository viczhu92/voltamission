Cross-border battery cell & BMS sourcing support for North American teams

VoltaMission helps US and Canadian manufacturers source cylindrical (18650, 21700) and pouch battery cells, and custom BMS, from global supply bases, including Asia, while reducing technical, communication, and quality risk.

What the service includes

  • Cell and BMS supplier discovery and technical shortlisting.
  • Engineering-led qualification of supplier capability and fit — cycle life, thermal-runaway margin, protection-circuit accuracy, and cell-balancing performance.
  • Bilingual English/Mandarin communication and negotiation support.
  • Overseas-side QC support and sample/cycle-test review coordination.
  • De-risked introductions to cell and BMS suppliers that match the client's technical requirements.

Why companies use it

Cross-border battery sourcing can fail because of language gaps, distance, unclear supplier identity, broker chains, sample-quality issues, or mismatched manufacturing capability. VoltaMission applies independent battery and BMS engineering judgment before a client commits to a supplier.
