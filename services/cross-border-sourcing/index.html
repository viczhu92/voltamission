Cross-border sourcing support for North American hardware teams

VoltaMission helps US and Canadian manufacturers source specialized components and equipment from global supply bases, including Asia, while reducing technical, communication, and quality risk.

What the service includes

  • Supplier discovery and technical shortlisting.
  • Engineering-led qualification of supplier capability and fit.
  • Bilingual English/Mandarin communication and negotiation support.
  • Overseas-side QC support and sample review coordination.
  • De-risked introductions to suppliers that match the client's technical requirements.

Why companies use it

Cross-border sourcing can fail because of language gaps, distance, unclear supplier identity, broker chains, sample-quality issues, or mismatched manufacturing capability. VoltaMission applies hands-on manufacturing and process-engineering judgment before a client commits to a supplier.
