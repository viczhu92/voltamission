Hard-to-find battery cell & BMS sourcing

VoltaMission locates cylindrical (18650, 4680) and pouch battery cells, and custom BMS, that distributors and marketplaces won't supply, for US and Canadian battery and EV hardware teams whose own network has hit a dead end — then qualifies the vendor on technical merit before an introduction.

What we source

  • Non-catalog and custom cylindrical (18650, 4680) and pouch cells not carried by standard distributors.
  • Allocation-gated cells that distributors won't release for low volumes.
  • Small-volume and R&D / prototype cell and BMS quantities that marketplaces ignore.
  • Custom BMS designs and components, including items made only overseas.

How it works

We identify the right cell or BMS, verify the vendor is a legitimate manufacturer, and qualify it on technical merit — cycle life, energy density, thermal-runaway margin, protection-circuit accuracy, samples, and origin compliance — before handing over a de-risked introduction. Sourcing spans North America and Asia, with bilingual English/Mandarin communication where needed.

Best-fit situations

This service is for hardware startups, EV builders, research groups, and manufacturers who have exhausted their contacts on a cell or BMS that is non-catalog, allocation-gated, small-volume, R&D-oriented, or only made overseas.

Outcome

Clients receive a qualified vendor and a de-risked path to the cell or BMS they couldn't source. VoltaMission holds no inventory and takes no margin on what it qualifies; the engagement is for engineering judgment and qualified introductions.
