01How it works

A qualification protocol, not a sales pitch.

The same four-step protocol our engineers used qualifying cells and BMS on production lines — applied to your supplier before you commit a purchase order.

STEP 01

Intake spec

Cell format (18650, 21700, pouch), target cycle life, energy density, and BMS protection requirements — captured before any vendor is contacted.

STEP 02

Certification & documentation audit

UN38.3 transport-safety and IEC 62133 cell-safety test reports, ISO 9001 / UL documentation, and factory audit records — verified against the actual report, not a claim on a spec sheet.

STEP 03

Sample & cycle-test verification

Physical samples checked against claimed cycle life, thermal-runaway margin, and — for BMS — protection-circuit accuracy and cell-balancing performance.

STEP 04

Qualified introduction

A de-risked handoff to the supplier — priced by quote or service fee, agreed before you commit, whatever form that engagement takes.

06FAQ

Battery & BMS sourcing questions, answered.

Where can I source 18650, 21700, or pouch cells when volumes are below a cell maker's direct-account threshold?

VoltaMission locates non-catalog and R&D through mid-production volume cylindrical (18650, 21700) and pouch cell suppliers that distributors and marketplaces won't handle — qualifying the vendor on technical merit and handing over a de-risked introduction across North America and Asia.

Why can't we get a direct account with a major cell manufacturer?

Tier-1 cell makers allocate capacity to automotive and grid-scale contracts with MOQs in the millions of cells and multi-year commitments. Below that threshold, you're routed to distributors and broker chains — where lot provenance, cell grade, and cert validity become your risk. VoltaMission qualifies the suppliers who will serve your volume, on the same technical criteria a tier-1 account would demand.

Can you qualify a second source for a cell we already use?

Yes. Second-source qualification — matching an incumbent cell's electrical and thermal behavior closely enough that your pack and BMS don't need redesign — is one of the most common engagements for teams in production. We verify against your incumbent's measured performance, not just its datasheet.

How do you qualify a custom BMS supplier beyond a datasheet?

We verify protection-circuit accuracy (over/under-voltage, over-current), cell-balancing performance, SOC/SOH estimation quality, and functional-safety documentation against real test data and samples — not marketing claims.

Are you a battery manufacturer or reseller?

Not a cell manufacturer. Depending on what a project needs, we engage as an independent qualification service, a sourcing or pack-build partner, or an authorized-channel component and equipment agent — the fee structure (quote or service fee) is agreed with you before you commit.

Do you also design and build battery packs, or act as an equipment agent?

Yes. Alongside cell and BMS qualification, we handle pack architecture, BMS integration, and assembly through production handoff, and we work as a sourcing partner or authorized agent for battery-manufacturing and laser equipment — priced by quote or service fee, depending on the engagement.

What certifications should a battery cell or BMS supplier have?

At minimum UN38.3 (transport safety) and IEC 62133 (cell/battery safety); UL 1642, UL 2054, or UL 2580 and ISO 9001 depending on application. We verify these against actual test reports, not a claim on a spec sheet.

How do North American teams source cells or BMS from Asia safely?

Technically qualified supplier shortlists, overseas-side QC, and bilingual (English / Mandarin) negotiation — removing the language, distance, and trust barriers.

Do you source battery test equipment, R&D materials, or laser equipment too?

Yes. Beyond cells and BMS, we source and qualify battery test and lab equipment (cyclers, environmental and thermal-safety test chambers), small-quantity R&D battery materials (electrode, electrolyte, separator), and laser equipment used in battery manufacturing — tab welding and cell-can cutting systems.

Need engineering eyes on a cell or BMS?

Tell us the cell format or BMS you need to qualify — and your target annual volume. We respond to qualified inquiries within two business days.
