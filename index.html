Our approach

Why this gap exists — and why it's hard to close.

The corridor isn't broken for lack of tools. It's broken for lack of trust, and lack of technical depth in the buyer's own voice.

North America has committed $200B+ to battery capacity. But the equipment, process know-how, and ramp-up experience it depends on sit in Asia.

Sourcing across that gap is broken — not for lack of discovery tools, but for lack of trust, and because buyers can't judge suppliers in their own engineering language. That is the gap we work in.
