About VoltaMission

Engineering-driven, founder-led.

Your counterpart will be an engineer who has been on real production floors – not a generic consultant learning battery manufacturing from a slide deck.

VoltaMission is led by battery manufacruting engineers who have worked across Asia and North America on lithium-ion programs – from lab equipment to full-scale production lines.

Experience includes process and equipment engineering, FAT for new tools, line bring-up, vision inspection, and data-driven yield work. The focus is simple: help your team make better technical decisions, faster, with less trial-and-error on the floor.
