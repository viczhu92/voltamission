About

Founded by Victor Zhu

VoltaMission was founded to bridge the gap between battery manufacturing expertise and the investment/business communities that need it.

With deep hands-on experience in lithium-ion battery production, from electrode preparation to cell formation, VoltaMission provides reality-checked insights for investors evaluating battery companies and actionable technical guidance for manufacturers scaling their operations.

Based in Ontario, Canada, VoltaMission serves clients across North America, with expertise spanning the full battery value chain including production processes, equipment selection, quality systems, and manufacturing data analytics.
